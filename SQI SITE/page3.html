ADMISSION INTO 2019/2020 ACADEMICS SESSION IS STILL AVAILABLE!

National Diploma admission for candidates who wrote the last 2018/2019 UTME and score 100 and above and are willing to change their Institution to SQI COllege of ICT can do so at the campus or by applying through the institution portal edu.sqi.ng
