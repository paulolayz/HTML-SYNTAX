Things you should know about JAMB Regularization

by Timi | Jan 6, 2022 | Uncategorized

The joint admissions and matriculations board (JAMB) is a Nigerian entrance examination board for tertiary-level institutions. The board conducts entrance unified tertiary matriculation examination for prospective undergraduates into Nigerian universities.


What is regularization in JAMB?

JAMB regularization is the process of ensuring that the JAMB board validates candidates current admission details with the school that offered admission to such candidates in their database with the aim of either granting one gateway of securing admission and granting admission letter/registration number.

The JAMB regularization procedure is designed to regulate ND/NID/OND, HND, and NCE type of admission to the university and clear up the JAMB status of a prospective student.

The purpose of the JAMB regularization is to ensure that JAMB has your current admission detail that tally with the admission/registration record in the school that offered you admission.

Application for JAMB regularization is also for students who don’t have an admission letter from JAMB but was offered admission in tertiary institutions.

Students who did remedial/pre-degree programme like basic programme or certificate programme or direct application to the school.

Students who did not register JAMB during the year they were offered admission (ie from basic and certificate programme).
This allows candidates with admission but with no prior record with JAMB ability to register on the platform.


What are the requirements for JAMB regularization?

1. JAMB result for an applicant who already has a valid JAMB registration number.

2. If you don’t have a JAMB number you are also eligible to apply for regularization.

3. O’level result, that is; WAEC, NECO, GCE, NABTEB or combined results.

4. Passport photograph.

5. Candidates personal details, that is; address, date of birth, email address, gender, Phone number, marital status, names, origin, previous JAMB registration number, year of enrolment.

6. Candidates’ institution details, that is; institution enrolled, reason for not being on JAMB list, matriculation number, matriculation year, year of graduation, serial no.

On graduating list, head of institution, registrar.

7. Current course studying in the institution.


Procedure to do JAMB regularization

1. Go to any nearest JAMB accredited centre or cybercafe with all the requirements stated above

2. Make payment and the JAMB representative will process your JAMB regularisation, confirm your information and complete the form.

3. Ensure to get printout after completion and submission of the form.

4. Submit the printed signed form to your school admission office.

5. The admission officer will submit it to JAMB office in your institution state.

6. Your institution will submit your form to the JAMB office in your state or at the JAMB headquarter.

7. You will get notification in your email address from JAMB.


How to check your JAMB regularization status?

1. Visit https://portal.JAMB.gov.ng/efacility../

2. Log in with your JAMB email and password.

3. After a successfully login, scroll down; locate and click the “check admission status” tab.

4. Select your exam year and enter your JAMB registration number in the required columns.

5. Lastly, click on “check admission status” to access your admission status.

If approved, proceed to print your admission letter.


UPDATE ON THE NATIONAL INNOVATION DIPLOMA PROGRAMME’S TUITION FEE STRUCTURE

by Timi | Feb 2, 2021 | Uncategorized

Decision Extract of the Senate Meeting held on 15th January 2021


As the institution continues to grow, it is deemed necessary to create a new fee structure, for the institution to maintain and enhance it’s operation and quality of ICT education delivery, hence the senate meeting to discuss the new fee structure for the national innovation programme at SQI College of ICT.

It is important to note that The National Board for Technical Education(NBTE) has its own curriculum that is enforced for all Diploma Students; while we also have a standard professional Certificate Programme curriculum for our students. The new fee structure makes it easier to administer both curriculums to students enrolled in the NID programme at SCICT, which can be taken simultaneously, as we seek to build students who stand out from the crowd.

During the course of the meeting, it was decided that;

  • Beginning From March 2021/2022 Academic session, Professional certificate Programmes for National Innovation Diploma (NID) Students will no longer be free at SQI College of ICT.
  • The Professional certificate Programme remains a requirement for graduation. This is what distinguishes SCICT students from students from other institutions.
  • The professional certificate programme can be taken in any of the campuses.
  • National Diploma activities hold only at the main campus.
  • Students don’t need to make payments for or take both programs at once. But all required courses must be taken and passed before graduation.
  • Current students taking professional courses can convert to a Diploma Program by paying an application and acceptance fee of N20,000 and N65,000 per semester.
  • It is compulsory for New students applying for the Diploma Programme to take the Professional certificate courses and make payments for both programmes.
  • The new fee structure shall take effect from March 2021 when the application for the new session will be commence.
  • The new fee structure does not affect the existing students.
  • Students may choose to run their Professional certificate courses electively (i.e, a student may come for Professional Programs during holiday instead of running it together with their academic works).

NID ( National Innovative Diploma) students are to receive two certificates, the NID certificate and the Professional Certificate after the completion of the program.

THE 10TH SQI ICT SCHOLARSHIP

by Timi | Oct 4, 2020 | Uncategorized

SQI College of ICT announces her 10th ICT scholarship. In a statement released by the rector of the college, Engineer Fredrick Aderinto, on the 1st of October. The Scholarship is expected to run from the 1st of October to the 31st of October.

SQI College of ICT has since 2008 been delivering training in ICT skills through world-class mentorship, employing an unconventional approach. The ever-increasing demand for professionally trained ICT personnel resulted in the launch the scholarship program in 2011 to open a wider window of opportunity to every promising talent, and today over 4336 students and professionals have benefited and since found a better life both in Nigeria and overseas.

It is also worthy of note that the registration for this year’s scholarship and the testing is absolutely free, as contained in the rector’s statement. The scholarship is open to anyone and everyone between the ages of 13 and 40. The tests scholarship test for this year has been slated to hold every Thursday and Friday between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM throughout the month of October. This year’s scholarship has also been structured to accommodate both on-site and online tests. The on-site test will take place at the college’s campus while the online test has been slated to take place on the last day of the scholarship program.

Interested candidates are enjoined to take hold of this opportunity and register in time to enjoy the aforementioned scholarship opportunity. To know more about the scholarship programme and to apply, visit the scholarship page on the college’s website.
