Success Story

The success of Paul Olayiwola is one that is so encouraging to many entering the field of coding. His love for coding and developing software began at a young age. He took particular interest in the well-known game, Minecraft ,specifically the education edition. The app was available on the school devices, and he used the built in coding blocks to create mods and understand how coding works. He enjoyed being able to program specific events, and he liked the process of troubleshooting faulty sequences to work the expected way. This seemingly whimsical experience was likely the sole reason Paul ever chose to pursue coding.

To learn more about Paul's experience with Minecraft,visit this page

Despite this early exposure, it was only until high school that Paul took a computer science course and learnt more about coding. Granted, he had previously done small projects for fun, learnt some things on the side, but none of those were intentional, long term sessions. The course Paul took was in the python language, and specified more on the basics of coding with python and styling things. It was a good experience for Paul, but after the course concluded, he felt like he hadn't learnt anything useful, and more importantly, anything he wanted to learn. He realized then that he cared less about designing things, and more about programming things--making things happen with a couple lines of code.

The following year, Paul's dad registered him for a course at SQI in Nigeria. This course was the web development course, which featured learning how to both design AND develop websites. Paul liked this and realized that it could be the very thing that he could do as a job in the future.

5 years later, Paul finished the course and went on to work as a freelance web developer, creating websites for several friends, family, and customers around the world.

3 years later, he would go on to create WideWeb, a training school specified for all things coding related for young children.
